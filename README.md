# Master's Thesis
This repo contains the `.tex` files and final copy of the Master's Thesis I submitted for the completion of my Master of Physics (Quantum Technology & Computing) degree in October 2025.

The complete thesis in pdf form is in `main.pdf`.

In short, the research in this thesis analysed the non-variational quantum walk-based optimisation algorithm and its performance at calculating the similarity between two network graphs.

## Notes (Added August 2026)
Later investigations that I carried out in early 2026 as part of my PhD were not able to verify a speedup in calculating graph similarity compared to simulated annealing. 
There is still a possibility that the exponential scaling of graph similarity calculations will allow this quantum approach to overtake the classical methods, but that trend has not been directly observed.
I will leave my thesis publicly available and unmodified to serve as a body of work demonstrating useful data analysis and simulation skills.
